A notebook written to the dead

What you can't say aloud, write to him

Marie Curie's private notebook to Pierre, 1906–07, published after her death

There are things you cannot say to a living person. Where those go. What she put in the notebook that year.

What actually happened?

For more than a year after Pierre's death she wrote in a notebook. Not a diary — words addressed to him: where she had been, what the children said, how she couldn't bear it, what she was supposed to do without him. The sentences are full of 'you'. She never showed it to anyone in her lifetime. After a year or so the entries thinned, and nowhere in it does she write that she needs to move on.

Write it to that person, not to anyone else

Said to the living it becomes a request for comfort; said to yourself it becomes analysis. Only addressed to them does the true thing come out — the undignified, unreasonable, repetitive parts. Who the letter is to decides what you can write.

Write their name at the top and use 'you'. Don't make it a record that someone else could read.

Mourning isn't a process due to finish

She set no deadline and never wrote that it was time to turn the page. The entries thinned by themselves; nothing was completed. Treat grief as a task with an end date and you spend it grieving and blaming yourself for still grieving.

Don't set a date by which you should be better. The day the writing thins will arrive on its own.

Nobody was going to read it, which is why it's honest

She kept it private for life. Precisely because it was for no one, she didn't have to be presentable, or reasonable, or brave. A notebook addressed only to the dead is one of the few places the living can be completely unperformed.

Find somewhere you're certain nobody will look. Not performing is the only requirement.

How do I use it today?

Where you are: there are things you can't say to anyone, and saying them gets you either comfort or advice, both of which make it worse.

Ask first: who are these words actually addressed to? Could I just write to them? Am I setting myself a date to be better by?

Where it goes wrong: reading it as 'writing makes you feel better' — she wrote for over a year and it didn't. This is about finding a place where the unsayable can be said honestly, not a treatment.

Lines to keep

A notebook for the dead is where the living need not perform.
