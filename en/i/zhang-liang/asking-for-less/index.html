Liu county is enough

Asking for the small thing at the summit

Records of the Grand Historian, Hereditary House of the Marquis of Liu

You are told to name your own reward. Do you take the largest one available? Here is why he took the smallest.

Zhang LiangAsking for the small thing at the summit

What actually happened?

With the empire settled, Liu Bang told him to choose thirty thousand households anywhere in Qi, the highest grant then possible. He declined. He had raised troops at Xiapi and met His Majesty at Liu, and that meeting was heaven's doing: enfeoff me at Liu and it is enough; I dare not accept thirty thousand households. Liu was a small county. He then pleaded illness, took up the regimen and gave up grain, saying he wished to drop the affairs of men. Of that founding generation Han Xin was killed and Peng Yue destroyed. Zhang Liang died in his bed.

Asking for the small thing is a signal

Thirty thousand households is not money, it is a base with its own revenue and its own men. What he turned down was the structure, not the wealth. Choosing a small county announces in public that he does not intend to grow into a problem somebody will eventually have to solve.

Winning the big account and then splitting it across two teams reads as generosity this quarter. It is really you declining to become the thing that has to be broken up.

Give the other side a version it can repeat

He did not say he was afraid of being suspected. He said Liu was where they met and that heaven had arranged it. The true reason spoken aloud would have shamed them both. Offering a version everyone can live with is not insincerity, it is leaving people room to move.

To get off a project, I think this will fail starts an argument. I am worth more on the other thing ends the same way and keeps the relationship intact.

Leave while people still want you to stay

He began withdrawing at the point of maximum credit and maximum usefulness. The timing and the proceeds of an exit run in opposite directions: the later you leave, the less there is to collect and the worse it looks going out the door.

Hand over while the numbers are good and the persuasion to stay is sincere. The identical handover after two bad quarters is a different event with a different name.

How do I use it today?

Where you are: something has gone well and a large reward is available for the asking.

Ask first: once you hold it, do you become a problem that someone above you has to manage? Say the answer out loud before you decide.

Where it goes wrong: converting every bit of credit into position; or, on the way out, stating the real reason so plainly that neither side can stay in the room.

Lines to keep

Enfeoff me at Liu. That is enough. I dare not take thirty thousand.

I wish to drop the affairs of men and follow Master Red Pine.

He declined not the money but a structure somebody would later dismantle.
