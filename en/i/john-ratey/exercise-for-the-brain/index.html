Exercise is for the brain

You can feel the return the same day

Spark; the Naperville school programme

Filing exercise under the gym leaves the cheapest medicine unused. What it's actually changing.

John RateyYou can feel the return the same day

What actually happened?

A high school in Naperville, outside Chicago, ran an experiment: move PE to first period, and stop measuring who runs fastest — students only had to hold their own high heart-rate zone. That cohort went on to score far above their peers in international maths and science assessments. Ratey uses it to show the link isn't mystical: exercise prompts the brain to release proteins that support neuron growth and connection, which he calls fertiliser for the brain. Exercise doesn't only shape the body. It shapes the brain.

Change the motive from your shape to today's state

Aim at your body and the return arrives months later, carried entirely by willpower. Aim at attention and mood and you can feel the return within hours. The second is real, and easier to sustain, because the feedback is fast.

Attention gone by mid-afternoon: twenty minutes of brisk walking usually beats another coffee, and borrows nothing.

Intensity matters more than duration

Naperville had each student hold their own high heart-rate zone rather than race each other. That's good news for ordinary people: you don't need long sessions, you need the heart rate to actually have gone up. Fifteen real minutes beats an hour of strolling.

With no time, do fifteen minutes that leaves you out of breath rather than an hour of turning up at the gym.

Put the hard thing after the exercise

If the window after exercise is when new connections form most readily, then schedule accordingly: the hardest learning or thinking goes after. Moving PE to first period was exactly this logic.

Before attacking a difficult document, move for twenty minutes. It saves time overall.

How do I use it today?

Where you are: your head won't turn over and you're forcing it.

Ask first: when did I last actually raise my heart rate? Is twenty minutes of moving a better trade than another hour of grinding?

Where it goes wrong: treating exercise as a cure-all and using it to replace the sleep you need and the problem you should be handling.

Lines to keep

The growth factors physical activity releases are fertiliser for the brain.

Schedule the hardest learning for after the exercise.
