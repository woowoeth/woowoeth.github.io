Winston ChurchillThe call came a decade later

What actually happened?

War broke out in September 1939 and he returned to the Admiralty. On 10 May 1940 he became Prime Minister, aged sixty-five. Writing later about that night, he described feeling as though his whole past life had been a preparation for this hour. The books written in the wilderness, the speeches on the record, the arguments rehearsed until fluent — all of it spent at once. He wasn't remembered. He was the one man who hadn't changed his line in ten years.

When the moment comes, it takes what's already in your hands

Nobody had time to develop anyone. He wasn't called because he had become likeable, but because on this subject he alone had a ready judgement, a ready record and a ready way of putting it. Being prepared means having it when someone else is in a hurry.

Ask: if someone needed it tomorrow, what could I hand over as it stands?

Sixty-five says the ledger isn't kept in years

By the standards of the day that was retirement age. What decided the matter was never the number of years but whether what he had built in that decade still existed and was still needed. Age only becomes the problem when your hands are empty.

Don't use age as the test. Count what's in your hands. That list decides whether you're still in time.

What you accumulate rarely gets used where you expected

He wrote to feed his family, laid brick to spend himself, and gathered figures for speeches nobody listened to. None of it was for the day that came, and all of it was spent on it. While accumulating you can't know where it lands. You can only make sure it's real.

It's fine that what you're doing has no visible use. Make sure it's a real craft and not just passing time.

How do I use it today?

Where you are: you've been building for a long time with no exit in sight, doubting whether any of it is still worth anything, or whether you're past the age.

Ask first: if someone needed it tomorrow, what could I hand over as it stands? Is what I've built a real craft? Are my judgements on the record with dates?

Where it goes wrong: reading it as 'hold on and your day comes' — most people's doesn't, and his had specific conditions: something ready, and a line he hadn't altered. It is not a licence to stop acting.
