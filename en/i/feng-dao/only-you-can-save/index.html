Only the emperor can save them

If you cannot change it, change the sentence

The Comprehensive Mirror, Annals of the Later Jin

The Khitan have destroyed the dynasty and the plain expects a massacre. What can a man with no army do in that room?

What actually happened?

The Later Jin fell, the Khitan ruler Yelu Deguang entered Kaifeng, and the population faced plunder and slaughter. Feng Dao went to see him. Yelu Deguang asked: the common people of the empire, how can they be saved? Feng Dao answered: at this moment the Buddha could come again and not save them; only the emperor can save them. The Comprehensive Mirror records that the people of the plain escaped destruction because of that one sentence. It contains no argument at all. It re-describes a decision as something only he is large enough to make.

When persuasion is impossible, change what the act is

Preaching mercy to a man who has just conquered you asks him to concede that he is in the wrong. Feng Dao argued nothing. He moved stopping the killing from you must back down to only you are able to. Same action, and one of them is a display of power.

Asking a dominant partner to soften terms on grounds of fairness does nothing. Describing the same concession as something only an operator of their size can afford runs at a different rate.

With no chips left, wording is the variable

He had no troops, no territory and nothing to trade. In an asymmetry that complete, the phrasing is the only thing still under his control. This is not a belief in clever talk. It is a reminder that even at zero leverage there remains one place where work can be done.

With no bargaining power, the one lever left is re-describing the options in front of the other side. It shifts nothing about strength and can still shift which option they take.

What he preserved was people, not his name

Going to meet the conqueror at all was enough to earn him centuries of contempt, and it duly did. He chose an outcome that can be counted, the number of people not killed, and gave up an abstract one that belonged to himself.

Compromising upwards to protect your team usually looks spineless from outside the team. Whether to do it depends on which of the two you are treating as the real result.

How do I use it today?

Where you are: the other side holds everything and you have nothing to trade.

Ask first: can the thing you want them to do be re-described as something only they are in a position to afford?

Where it goes wrong: arguing principle when you hold no cards; or treating wording as generally powerful, when it is the last resort available only after the substantive options are gone.

Lines to keep

The Buddha could come again and not save them. Only the emperor can.

The people of the plain were spared because of one sentence.

Keep the square inch of the heart free of evil and stand among tigers.
