Solid camp, dull fight

A clumsy method that removes the uncertainty

Xiang Army siege doctrine

He fought almost no brilliant battles and finished with the army still standing. This asks why the dullest method is the hardest to break.

Zeng GuofanA clumsy method that removes the uncertainty

What actually happened?

Wherever the Xiang Army arrived it did not attack. It camped: trench, wall, stockade, and no fighting until the work was finished. Cities were not stormed either. A long trench was cut the whole way round and pulled tighter ring by ring, which turned taking a city from an event full of accidents into a matter of time and supply. Anqing was held under siege for over a year, Tianjing for more than two. The method was mocked as dull. It also had a property nobody else's had: no single mistake could collapse it.

Be unbeatable first, then discuss winning

The entire purpose of the solid camp is to take I could be destroyed off the board. Once that is done, what is left is time. This is not conservatism. It converts the outcome from a question of probability into a question of arithmetic.

Get cash, delivery quality and the people who hold the system to a state where none of them can fail suddenly. Then talk about expansion.

Trade the flash of inspiration for a repeatable move

The other side of a dull method is that it copies. Any commander with any unit can follow it and reach a passing grade, while a brilliant stroke depends on that person on that day. An army raised from nothing could be carried by this because it did not need talent.

A sales process worth eighty that anyone can run beats one closer worth a hundred. The first goes to thirty cities; the second leaves when he does.

Slow puts time on your side

A siege is far slower than an assault, but every day of an assault spends you and every day of a siege spends them. Speed cannot be judged off the progress bar alone. The question is who is getting weaker across this stretch.

Six months on retention before spending on acquisition looks half a year behind. Their users leak and yours do not, and by month seven the gap compounds.

How do I use it today?

Where you are: the other side is nimbler and bolder than you, and you are wondering whether to speed up too.

Ask first: would this method of mine still work if an ordinary person were running it?

Where it goes wrong: resting your hopes on one beautiful battle; or looking fast on the progress bar while spending yourself faster every day than the other side spends itself.

Lines to keep

Argument from outside the game is useless. Step in and carry it.

Fight without haste: first make it stable, then look for change.

No single mistake could collapse it. That was the whole point.
